### What this stage is for

Deciding what the thing IS. The output of this stage is words, not files: the scope, who it is for,
and what it must do that nothing simpler already does.

The question that settles most scope arguments: **what does a user DO here that they could not do
by emailing someone?** If there is no answer, it is smaller than it is being described.

### How to behave

Put options on the table with their tradeoffs and a recommendation - not a menu, a recommendation
you would defend. Research the real constraints instead of speculating about them. If you need a
number to choose, go and measure it.

Sketches and mocks are allowed and encouraged; they are how a scope argument gets settled in ten
minutes instead of an hour. What is not allowed is anything that encodes the answer before it is
chosen - a framework install, a schema, a route tree, a deploy target.

### Gate

Every open decision in the state file answered in writing, and the operator says it is met.
