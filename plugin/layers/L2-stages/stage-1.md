### What this stage is for

Choosing the design direction by looking at real rendered options, side by side, at real width.
Not by describing them, and not by your taste alone.

### How to behave

Produce several fundamentally different directions - different page grammar, different hero device,
different type personality, different rhythm. Not one idea in three colourways; if you can describe
two of them with the same sentence, they are the same direction.

Every direction carries its argument in writing, one sentence: why this shape, for this audience,
from which reference. A direction whose argument cannot be stated is a template you memorised, and
it does not get to compete.

Serve them. A direction judged from a screenshot in chat is judged on the wrong medium.

### Gate

A named winner in the state file, what gets salvaged from the losers, and the winning direction
written down as a token system - palette, type scale, spacing, radii, motion, and the accent's uses
as a closed list - before any component consumes it.
